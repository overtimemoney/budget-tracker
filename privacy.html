Privacy Policy — My Budget

Effective November 17, 2026

My Budget is a personal budgeting app that runs entirely in your web browser. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does with it. The short version: your financial data stays on your device, and nothing is sold, shared, or sent to the developer.

Data stored on your device

The transactions, budgets, recurring expenses, and settings you use in the app are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. The app has no account system and no server that receives your financial data. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Optional Gmail sync

If you choose to connect Gmail in the app's Settings, the app requests read-only access to your Gmail account (the gmail.readonly scope) and uses it for one purpose only: finding transaction alert emails from Venmo, Capital One, and Bank of America so it can turn them into budget entries automatically.

  • Email messages are fetched directly from Google to your device and processed on your device. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • The access token Google issues is kept in memory only while the app is open. It is never stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere other than Google's own APIs.
  • The app never asks for your bank login, and it never connects to your bank. It reads only the alert emails described above.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and you can revoke the app's access completely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data sharing

None. The app does not sell, rent, share, or disclose any personal or financial data to anyone, for any purpose.

Your choices

You can export or permanently erase all of the app's data at any time from Settings. Erasing removes everything stored on the device, including imported transactions and settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
